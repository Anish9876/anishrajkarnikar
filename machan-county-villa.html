Machan County Villa

A responsive resort website that showcases the villa’s accommodations and natural surroundings, with intuitive navigation and a mobile-first experience.

RoleDesigner & Developer
ScopeEnd to end
Duration2 months
Built withBootstrap
Overview

A resort site that feels like the place.

Machan County Villa is a resort set in natural surroundings. The website needed to present its accommodations clearly, convey the atmosphere of the location, and make it easy for visitors to explore on any device.

I designed and developed the full site end to end, from structure and layout through to the responsive build.

Goals

What the site had to do.

  • Showcase the accommodations and the natural surroundings.
  • Make navigation intuitive so guests find rooms and information quickly.
  • Work smoothly on mobile, where most travellers browse.
Process

From idea to launch in two months.

01

Understand

Define the resort’s story, audience and the content the site must present.

02

Structure

Plan pages and navigation so key information is never more than a tap away.

03

Design

Create layouts that let imagery of the villa and its surroundings lead.

04

Build

Develop the responsive front end with Bootstrap and test across devices.
